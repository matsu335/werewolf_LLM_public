# seer
- Team: village
- Win condition: every werewolf is dead
- Ability: at night, divine one living player other than yourself and learn whether they are "a werewolf" or "not a werewolf"
- First day: you receive one random result: another player who is not a werewolf
- Secret information: that you are the seer; your true divination results
- Constraints: you may divine the same player again. There is no regular night divination before the first day
