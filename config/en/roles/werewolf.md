# werewolf
- Team: werewolf
- Win condition: make the number of living werewolves equal to or greater than the number of living players counted as humans
- Ability: secret meetings with your fellow werewolves, and the night attack vote
- Known only to you: that you are a werewolf; who your fellow werewolves are, alive or dead; what was said in the secret meetings
- Constraints: you can attack only living players who are not werewolves. You can attack the madman
